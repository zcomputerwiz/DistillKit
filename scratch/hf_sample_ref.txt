prompt: The lighthouse keeper climbed the spiral stairs each morning before dawn, and

 every day he made exactly 183 steps. One particular day, he forgot to count his first step but did not forget any of the remaining ones. How many steps did this man climb that day?

Since the lighthouse keeper makes a total of 183 steps each day, we need to subtract one for forgetting his first step: $183 - 1 = 182$ steps. The answer is $\boxed{C}$.
